About Erica Love

I became interested in AI by first becoming interested in all the work people should never have been doing manually.

Agent First began with a practical observation: organizations ask people to be fast, accurate, compliant, empathetic, consistent, and adaptable while forcing them to reconstruct the decision environment from fragmented systems in real time.

The person searches. Remembers. Switches tabs. Interprets the rule. Checks the exception. Finds the approved language. Asks a supervisor. Rebuilds context. Then everyone calls the result “performance.”

That led me to a different question: What if a meaningful share of the performance problem is actually a system-design problem?

From there, Agent First grew beyond a single product idea. It became a way of looking at work: identify what the human is being required to carry, decide what responsibility the surrounding system should absorb, preserve human control where judgment matters, and test whether the redesign actually improves the work.

My consulting practice applies that thinking to messy operations, emerging AI initiatives, knowledge-heavy workflows, and founders who can see an opportunity but need help turning it into a coherent system.

What I am — and am not — claiming

Human-centered system design is not new. The Agent First synthesis is mine.

The lineage

Agent First sits in conversation with sociotechnical systems, human factors, human-computer interaction, distributed cognition, service design, knowledge management, and decision-support research. All of these traditions challenge technology-first thinking in different ways.

The contribution I am developing

A practical language for the AI era: the human/system responsibility split, the seven-layer decision environment, retrieval as labor, the supervisor tax, meaningful human control, and proof-before-scale as an operating discipline.

I would rather build a durable point of view on top of real intellectual foundations than pretend every useful idea appeared with the latest model release.

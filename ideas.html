The Agent First point of view

Work is already a system. AI changes who can carry the burden.

Agent First is a way of thinking about work before it is a piece of software. The central question is not “Where can we put AI?” It is: What should the person be responsible for, what should the surrounding system be responsible for, and where does AI actually improve that division?

Six signature ideas

The territory I am building around.

01 · WORK DESIGN

AI transformation is a work-design problem.

If you layer AI onto a badly designed workflow, you may speed up fragments without improving the system. Redesign the work first: decisions, information, responsibilities, handoffs, and exceptions. Then decide what AI belongs inside it.

Core thesis
02 · RETRIEVAL

Retrieval is work.

Searching, remembering, switching tabs, reconstructing context, finding the right version of a rule, and translating policy into action are not neutral background activities. They consume time and cognition. A good system treats that burden as designable.

Operational burden
03 · MANAGEMENT

The interface is part of the operating model.

What a screen shows, hides, sequences, explains, or requires a person to remember changes how work is performed. Interface design is therefore not cosmetic. It distributes responsibility, discretion, and risk.

Product + operations
04 · HUMAN CONTROL

“Human in the loop” is not a responsibility strategy.

A person clicking approve after an opaque system has already framed the decision is not meaningful control. Human control requires visibility, explanation, authority, and a deliberate answer to what judgment the person still owns.

Human-centered AI
05 · SYSTEM DEBT

The supervisor tax is often system debt.

When frontline ambiguity is unresolved, it gets pushed upward as repeat questions, interruptions, approvals, coaching, and escalation. Supervisors become the runtime patch for a system that has not encoded what the organization already knows.

Management systems
06 · VALIDATION

Proof should come before scale.

AI ambition should not outrun operational evidence. A bounded pilot should prove that the redesigned system improves something that matters—speed, accuracy, confidence, adoption, cost, customer experience, or risk—before integration expands.

Pilot discipline
Intellectual lineage

Agent First is a new synthesis, not a claim that human-centered systems were invented yesterday.

The work sits in conversation with long-running traditions that study people and technology as one system. What I am developing is an applied operating model for the AI era: a practical language for responsibility, decision environments, operational burden, human control, and proof.

Human-computer interactionInteraction design changes behavior, comprehension, confidence, and control.
Distributed cognitionKnowledge and reasoning do not live only in a person; they are distributed across people, artifacts, tools, and environment.

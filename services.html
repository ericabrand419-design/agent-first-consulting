Services

I work in the messy middle between “something is wrong” and “we know what to build.”

Agent First Consulting combines work design, product thinking, knowledge architecture, and applied AI. The engagement begins with the real system of work—not a predetermined software answer.

01 · Diagnose

Work System Diagnostic

$2,500–$5,000 indicative
Start here

Find where the work is creating unnecessary decision burden, search, ambiguity, context switching, handoffs, or supervisor dependence.

We examine

Actors, goals, systems, rules, exceptions, information flow, decisions, scripts, escalation, failure modes, and where people are compensating for the environment.

You receive

A system map, friction and risk analysis, responsibility split, prioritized redesign opportunities, and a recommended next experiment.

02 · Define

Concept → System Sprint

$5,000–$12,500 indicative

Turn a promising but messy product, service, internal tool, or AI idea into a coherent system someone can actually understand and test.

Typical work

Problem framing, user model, workflows, system responsibilities, information architecture, interaction model, knowledge needs, guardrails, and prototype direction.

You receive

A clear concept architecture, key flows, product requirements, prototype or prototype brief, decision log, and build roadmap.

03 · Redesign

AI Work Redesign & Readiness

$7,500–$20,000+ indicative

Decide what AI should retrieve, explain, generate, recommend, automate—or stay out of—after the work itself is understood.

Questions we answer

What remains human-owned? What becomes system-owned? Where is deterministic logic safer? What evidence must AI show? What can be overridden? What requires escalation?

You receive

An AI responsibility map, human-control boundaries, workflow recommendations, risk and governance requirements, prioritized use cases, and pilot plan.

04 · Prove

Prototype + Pilot Design

Scope-based

Move from a good idea to a bounded real-world test with a baseline, measurement plan, operating rules, and explicit scale gate.

Can include

Prototype design, test-mode workflows, knowledge structure, pilot cohort definition, observation plan, analytics, user testing, governance, and iteration loops.

The goal

Answer the question that matters before a large implementation: does the redesigned system measurably improve the work?

05 · Build alongside you

Design Partner / Advisory

Custom engagement

For founders or teams who need an ongoing partner across product definition, workflow design, applied AI, prototyping, pilot learning, and operating-model decisions.

Best fit

You have a complicated idea or changing system and need someone who can move between strategy, user experience, operations, product architecture, and evidence.

Not staff augmentation

The value is synthesis: turning ambiguity into a model, making the model tangible, and continually testing whether the system deserves to become more complex.

Where I am especially useful

Complexity that crosses product, operations, and human behavior.

Knowledge-heavy operations

People must navigate rules, procedures, exceptions, and client or situation-specific context while acting in real time.

Half-formed product ideas

The founder or team can feel the opportunity but needs the system, user, flows, responsibilities, and proof plan made explicit.

AI initiatives with unclear boundaries

The technology is possible, but nobody has yet decided what the AI should own, what the human should own, or how success will be measured.
